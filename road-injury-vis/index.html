Beyond the Road Toll

Who ends up in hospital after Australian road crashes, 2011–2021

Trend Road users Age & sex States First Nations

Road safety news usually counts deaths, but far more people are hospitalised after road crashes. This site explores who is hospitalised in Australia and how this changed from 2011 to 2021, with a focus on motorcyclists, pedal cyclists and pedestrians.

Note: the data are counts of hospital admission cases after road crashes, not crashes or deaths. Raw counts are not adjusted for population size.
