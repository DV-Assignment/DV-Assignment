National trend

How did the number of hospitalisations after road crashes in Australia change from 2011 to 2021, and which changes reflect real trends rather than the breaks in the series caused by changes in Victoria (from 2012) and New South Wales (from June 2017)?

What the data says

Cases went from 34,033 in 2011 to 39,505 in 2021, a rise of about 16%. Most of the growth happened between 2013 and 2016. In 2020 cases fell by 4.8%, then rose again in 2021.

Keep in mind

Victoria (from July 2012) and New South Wales (from June 2017) stopped counting people treated only in emergency departments as admissions. These changes lower the counts after each line, so the real rise may be larger than shown.
