What the data says

First Nations people injured on the road are younger: in 2021, 31% were aged 17 to 25, compared with 20% of non-Indigenous people. A larger share were pedestrians (10.6% compared with 5.7%), and a smaller share were pedal cyclists (9.3% compared with 21.6%).

Keep in mind

Each bar is a share of that group's own cases. The chart compares the profile of who is injured, not how many more people in one group are injured than in the other.
