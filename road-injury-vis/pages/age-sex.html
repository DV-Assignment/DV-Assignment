What the data says

In 2021, 65% of cases were male (25,788 males and 13,716 females). The 40 to 64 age group had the most cases for both sexes. The gap is widest for motorcyclists: 89% of injured motorcyclists were male.

Keep in mind

These are case counts, not rates. Age groups cover different numbers of years and people, so a bigger bar does not always mean a higher risk.
