Road users

Which road user groups are hospitalised most, and did the share of motorcyclists, pedal cyclists and pedestrians grow between 2011 and 2021?

What the data says

Motorcyclists, pedal cyclists and pedestrians grew from 46.2% of cases in 2011 to 49.1% in 2021. Pedal cyclists grew the most, from 15.8% to 20.7%.

Keep in mind

These are shares, not counts. A group's share can grow because it had more cases, or because other groups had fewer. Hover a year to see the total number of cases behind the bands.
