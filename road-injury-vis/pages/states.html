What the data says

Raw counts make the big states look worst, but rates tell a different story. In 2021 the Northern Territory had the highest rate (286 per 100,000), more than twice the rate of New South Wales (111). In the ACT, two in three injured people (67%) were motorcyclists, pedal cyclists or pedestrians.

Keep in mind

Cases are counted by the state where the person lives, not where the crash happened. The states and territories add up to slightly less than the Australian total.
